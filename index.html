Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our royal Jaipur recipes, ingredients, and distributorship partnerships.

What makes ShahiCrunch Jaipur's royal ice cream brand? ▼

ShahiCrunch is inspired by royal Rajasthani dessert traditions. We use authentic Kashmiri saffron, Belgian dark chocolate, and California almonds, slow-churned in pure vegetarian milk fat without palm oil or artificial preservatives.

What are the 4 signature flavors available? ▼

Our signature quartet includes Kesar Pista (pure saffron & roasted pistachios), Chocolive (Belgian cocoa with chocolate curls), Double Crunch (golden wafer fudge & hazelnuts), and Dry Fruit Raita (Jaipur's traditional sweet curd reimagined).

How can I apply for ShahiCrunch distributorship in Rajasthan or Pan-India? ▼

We offer attractive profit margins, dedicated -24°C cold-chain delivery logistics, and full marketing backing. You can apply on our Distributorship page or fill the inquiry form below.

Where is ShahiCrunch located and what are the operating hours? ▼

Our headquarters is located at MI Road, C-Scheme, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302001. We are open for inquiries and tastings Monday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 11:30 PM.
