# ShahiCrunch Gateway

Create a professional lead-generation website for ShahiCrunch, an ice cream brand based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. The website should be premium, modern, mobile-responsive, and focused on generating distributor inquiries, retailer partnerships, and customer leads.

Brand Name: ShahiCrunch
Industry: Ice Cream & Frozen Desserts
Location: Jaipur, Rajasthan

Featured Products:
- Kesar Pista Ice Cream
- Double Crunch Ice Cream
- Chocolive Ice Cream
- Dry Fruit Raita

Pages:
1. Home
2. About Us
3. Products
4. Distributor Inquiry
5. Testimonials
6. Contact Us

Homepage Sections:
- Hero banner with premium ice cream imagery and tagline: 'Royal Taste in Every Crunch'
- Featured products showcase
- Why Choose ShahiCrunch section
- Distributor opportunity section
- Customer testimonials
- Contact form and WhatsApp CTA

Lead Generation Features:
- Inquiry form (name, phone, email, city, message)
- Distributor application form
- Sticky WhatsApp button
- Click-to-call button

Website Copy:
Headline: 'Experience the Royal Taste of ShahiCrunch'
Subheadline: 'Premium ice creams crafted with rich flavors, quality ingredients, and unforgettable taste.'
CTA Buttons: 'Become a Distributor', 'Request a Callback', 'Explore Flavors', 'Contact Us'

Design Preferences:
- Premium and appetizing visual style
- Colors: Royal Gold, Cream White, Chocolate Brown
- Elegant typography
- High-quality product imagery
- SEO-friendly structure

Include responsive design, fast-loading pages, local SEO for Jaipur, and strong calls-to-action throughout the site.
